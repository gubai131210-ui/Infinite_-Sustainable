# 传统文化 · 水墨山水（只讲画）

改编自 [AI Tools Guidebook 水墨山水 Prompt](https://aitoolsguidebook.com/zh/articles/chinese-ink-landscape-prompts/)

## 必写

1. `monochrome ink, no color`
2. `generous negative space`（建议写清 60–70% empty / top half empty）
3. **显式三远平面**：Far / Middle / Near
4. `vertical scroll composition` + `rice paper texture`
5. 可加一枚 `small red seal stamp`（唯一彩色）
6. **禁止** chinoiserie 云纹边框、龙纹铺满、纹饰顶替山水语言

## 主模板（封面 / 壁纸共用，改 Near 细节即可）

```text
SUBJECT: traditional Chinese ink landscape explaining spatial depth (Song dynasty influence), NOT a copy of any named museum masterpiece
STYLE: ink wash, sumi-e, calligraphic brush, rice paper fiber texture, monochrome ink only
LIGHTING: soft paper-ambient, no studio flash, no cinematic color grade
COMPOSITION: vertical 3:4 hanging-scroll feel; explicit three planes —
  Far: pale mountain silhouettes barely visible;
  Middle: a mist band dissolving form;
  Near: detailed pine and rock with confident dark strokes;
  top ~55% mostly negative space
MOOD: quiet, contemplative, archival, literati calm
TECHNICAL: ultra-detailed brush edges, high resolution, clean paper grain

NEGATIVE: color, saturation, warm tint, chinoiserie, ornamental cloud-scroll frame, dragon motif, textile pattern border, photoreal leaves, glossy stone, western oil painting, watermark, text, logo, collage
```

## 变体：平远江景

```text
minimal Chinese ink wash, lone boat on a wide river, soft fog dissolving the far shore, mountains barely visible, sumi-e, 70% negative space, vertical composition, monochrome ink, rice paper texture
NEGATIVE: color, chinoiserie borders, decorative patterns, photoreal
```
